## Who to call (N11 & non-emergency numbers)

N11 codes are the "short dial" for community and government services &mdash; just dial the number.

- **[2-1-1](tel:211) &mdash; Community Services:** Central Michigan 211 (United Way of Genesee County) connects you to food pantries, housing assistance (Shelter of Flint), and utility help (GCCARD).
- **[5-1-1](tel:511) &mdash; Travel & Traffic:** MDOT Mi Drive &mdash; real-time I-69 / I-475 construction and winter road conditions.
- **[7-1-1](tel:711) &mdash; Relay Service:** Michigan Relay for those who are deaf, hard of hearing, or speech-disabled.
- **[8-1-1](tel:811) &mdash; MISS DIG:** Michigan's utility safety line. Call at least 3 business days before digging in your yard.
- **[9-1-1](tel:911) &mdash; Emergency:** Genesee County 911 &mdash; dispatch for Burton Police, Fire, and EMS.
- **[9-8-8](tel:988) &mdash; Crisis Lifeline:** connects you to Genesee Health System (the local mental-health authority) for crisis support and behavioral-health urgent care.

### Key local non-emergency numbers

- **Burton Police (non-emergency):** [(810) 742-2542](tel:+18107422542) &mdash; crimes not in progress, noise complaints, general info
- **Burton DPW:** [(810) 742-9230](tel:+18107429230) &mdash; potholes, water main breaks, snow removal

## Addiction & recovery resources

> **If you are in immediate danger or a medical crisis, call [911](tel:911) or the National Suicide
> and Crisis Lifeline at [988](tel:988).**

You're not struggling alone.

**[Narcotics.com](https://www.narcotics.com/na-meetings/michigan/burton/)** connects people to free
resources, patient-centered guidance, and verified Narcotics Anonymous meetings nationwide &mdash;
including a dedicated **[NA meetings page for the City of Burton](https://www.narcotics.com/na-meetings/michigan/burton/)**.
Help line: **[1-800-934-1582](tel:+18009341582)**.

### Other trusted partners

- **[Therapy.com](https://www.therapy.com/)** &mdash; connects users with licensed therapists nationwide; a searchable directory plus mental-health and therapy resources.
