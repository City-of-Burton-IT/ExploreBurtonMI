## Welcome to the City of Burton

Welcome, neighbor! The City of Burton, formerly Burton Township, was established in 1972.
Through the years our city has grown in many ways, making it a great place to live, work,
and raise your family.

This guide is your one-stop shop for City of Burton information — administrative staff,
city council and meeting schedules, waste pickup, recycling, and permits for burning and
dumping. If you have questions, contact City Hall Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to
5:00 p.m. at (810) 743-1500.

*— Mayor Duane Haskins*

## City Offices

**Department of Public Works (DPW)** — 4093 Manor Dr., Burton, MI 48519
Hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m. – 4:00 p.m.
