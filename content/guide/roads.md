## Live traffic & construction (state routes)

For real-time lane closures, construction, and traffic on the **state highways** through and
around Burton — **I-69**, **I-475**, and **M-54 (Dort Highway)** — use MDOT's live map:

- **[MDOT Mi Drive live traffic & construction map](https://mdotjboss.state.mi.us/MiDrive/map)**

Mi Drive covers Interstate, US, and M (state) routes only — **not** city or county streets.

## City street projects

The City maintains local roads through its **Major Streets** and **Local Streets** funds (see the
**Finances** dashboard in this app for how those are budgeted). The **Fiscal Year 2026-2027 budget**
includes road, intersection, and sidewalk work along these corridors:

- **Belsay Road**, **Bristol Road**, **Maple Avenue**, **Genesee Road** (incl. a sidewalk project),
  **Court Street**, **North Grand Traverse Street**, **Covert Road**, and **Hemphill Road**
- Intersection upgrades at **Bristol/Belsay** (roundabout) and **Bristol/Fenton**
- The **Iron Belle Trail** non-motorized path

These are budgeted projects, not a live closure schedule, and their timing can shift. For the
status of a current local road project or to report a road concern, contact the **Department of
Public Works** at **(810) 742-9230**
([DPW page](https://burtonmi.gov/departments/department_of_public_works/index.php)).

## Neighborhood paving

Several neighborhoods are repaved through **special-assessment paving districts** funded by the
benefiting property owners. Questions about a paving district or assessment: contact City Hall at
**(810) 743-1500**.
