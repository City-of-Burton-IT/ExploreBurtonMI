## Burton Code of Ordinances

The City's local laws are collected in the **Code of Ordinances**, published and kept up to date
for the City by **American Legal Publishing**. The online code is the official, always-current
version and includes a search box for finding a specific rule.

- **[Browse the full Code of Ordinances](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/burtonmi/latest/burton_mi/0-0-0-1)**

## Certified copies & recent ordinances

The online code is updated after the City Council adopts and codifies an ordinance, so the newest
actions may not appear there yet. For a certified copy of an ordinance, or the status of a recent
one, contact the **City Clerk** at City Hall, **(810) 743-1500**.
