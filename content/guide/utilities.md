## Utility Billing

The Treasurer's Office at City Hall handles water, sewer, tax, and invoice billing.
Call **(810) 743-1500** with questions.

## Automatic Bill Payment

The City offers **automatic bill payment** for utility customers — a convenient way to
pay your utility bill on time and avoid delinquent fees, at no cost to you. Payments are
deducted from your checking or savings account on the **15th** of the month when your
balance is due.

To enroll, complete the **Utility Payment Automatic Transfer Authorization** form and
return it to:

> City of Burton, 4303 S. Center Rd., Burton, MI 48519

You can get the authorization form from the Treasurer's Office or in the official Resident
Packet (use the "Download the official packet (PDF)" link in this guide). For details,
call the City Treasurer's Office at **(810) 743-1500**.
