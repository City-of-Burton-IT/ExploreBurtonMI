## Register, check your status, and find where to vote

Michigan's official **Voter Information Center** is the one place to handle almost everything about
voting — registration, your polling location, your sample ballot, and absentee ballots:

- **[Michigan Voter Information Center (MVIC)](https://mvic.sos.state.mi.us/)** — register or update
  your address, check your registration, find your polling place, view your **sample ballot**, apply
  for an **absentee ballot**, and track your ballot. For current and upcoming **election dates**, see
  MVIC (dates change, so we link the official source rather than list them here).

Michigan offers same-day registration: you can register in person at your **city clerk's office**
through Election Day with proof of residency.

## Vote by mail (absentee)

Any registered Michigan voter may request an absentee ballot — no reason required. Request it
through **[MVIC](https://mvic.sos.state.mi.us/)** or directly from the Burton City Clerk, then return
your completed ballot to the Clerk by mail or to a designated drop box. Track its status on MVIC.
