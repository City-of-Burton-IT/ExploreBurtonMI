## City parks

Find any of these on the **Map** (filter by *Parks & Recreation*):

- **Kelly Lake Park** — 40-acre park off Lapeer Rd with lake access, nature trails, a picnic
  pavilion, and a bike path.
- **Settlement Park** — passive linear park along Thread Creek with fishing and picnic areas.
- **For-Mar Nature Preserve & Arboretum** — 380-acre nature preserve with trails and an
  arboretum (operated by [Genesee County Parks](https://www.geneseecountyparks.org)).
