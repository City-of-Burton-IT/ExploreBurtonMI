## What to expect on city streets

The City of Burton Department of Public Works (DPW) Road Division clears city streets to the
standard of **"safe roads at safe speeds" — not bare roads.** Streets are salted and/or sanded so
you can travel safely at a safe speed, but **you should still expect snow on the road during a
storm.** Please drive for the conditions.

Most plowing and salting happens during the day, when most people are traveling — roughly
**5:00 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.** Expect **reduced coverage in the evening and overnight**, and varying
road conditions, and drive accordingly.

## Which roads get cleared first

To treat the busiest roads first, the city works in priority order:

- **Priority 1 — Major roads, plus dangerous hills, curves, and intersections.** Cleared first.
- **Priority 2 — Local / neighborhood streets.** Addressed after Priority 1 roads are done.

For larger storms (over 4 inches), the city is divided into **7 areas** and crews are deployed to
**all 7 at once**, so every part of the city is being treated at the same time.

## How long clearing takes

Timelines depend on how much snow falls. These are targets for a **single** storm — if a new storm
starts before crews finish, the clock resets.

| Total snowfall | What crews do | Time to finish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 in | Salt Priority 1 roads as they cover | (ongoing) |
| 2–4 in | Plow all lanes of Priority 1 roads | up to 24 hours |
| 4–8 in | Team-plow Priority 1, then Priority 2 (12-hour operations) | about 72 hours |
| 8–12 in | As above for 4+ in events | about 96 hours |
| over 12 in | As above for 4+ in events | about 120 hours |

## Snow emergencies & parking

During a snow emergency the city may issue a **no-parking advisory** so crews can clear neighborhood
streets — parked cars block the plows. When that happens, the Mayor's office sends a press release to
local **TV and radio, the Burton View, the Flint Journal / MLive,** and posts it on the
**[city website](https://www.burtonmi.gov)**. Please move vehicles off the street when an advisory is
in effect.

## What's on the roads

- **Salt** is the primary material — it keeps snow and ice from bonding to the pavement. Salt works
  down to about **15°F**; below that (and not warming), sand is used for temporary traction.
- **Winter sand** provides traction at **curves, intersections,** and trouble spots during icy
  conditions, and at accident scenes where salt would work too slowly.

*Source: City of Burton DPW Road Division Snow and Ice Control Plan (revised December 2016). This is
