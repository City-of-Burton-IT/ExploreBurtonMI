## Dump Permits

Dump permits (with full regulations) are available at **Burton City Hall**. Each Burton
household is entitled to **one (1) free dump permit per month**. You must reside in the
household as your principal residence and show **proof of residency** (valid ID, water
bill). Only Burton residents may request a permit, and it must be obtained at City Hall.

- A permit covers materials from your own home only — proper ID is required at the landfill.
- One permit equals a standard pickup-truck load, **under 1,000 lbs**. Larger loads are
  charged the difference (and you may be asked to drive back). A **reflective vest** is
  required to enter the landfill.
- Good for small amounts of household demolition and household refuse. Michigan law
  requires a **tarp** over each load. You must be fully unloaded and out before closing.
- Freon must be removed by a licensed company and tagged with the removal date.
- If you can't use the permit within the calendar month, return it to City Hall for a new
  one. **Lost permits are not replaced.**

**Disposal site:** Brent Run Landfill, 8335 Vienna Rd., Montrose, MI 48457 — open
**Monday–Friday, 7:00 a.m. – 3:00 p.m.** Questions: (810) 639-3077. (The landfill is
outside the city, so it isn't pinned on the map — use the map's "Dump Permits" listing for
directions.)

## Burning Permits

It is **illegal to burn in the City of Burton without a permit.** Permits are issued for
one- and two-family residential dwellings only (no commercial), and your driver's license
must match the burning address. Get permits from the Fire Department, City Hall, or
[burtonmi.gov](https://www.burtonmi.gov). Keep your permit on hand whenever you burn.

**General rules:** keep an adequate fire line around the burn area, always attend the fire,
and keep a garden hose or other means of extinguishment on hand. **Barrel burning is not
permitted.** Don't burn when it's windy. Smoke and ash must not be a nuisance to neighbors.

### Open Burning

- Brush, tree limbs, and garden debris only — **no leaves, grass, or building materials.**
- **Four (4)** three-consecutive-day open-burn permits per household per year.
- Stay **50 feet** from any building or structure; pile no larger than 4 ft × 5 ft.
- **Fires must be out by sundown.** Extensions may be granted by calling the Fire
  Department or City Hall within 30 days of issuance.

### Recreational Burning

- **Seasoned wood only** — no leaves, grass, garden debris, or building materials.
- **Annual** permit, good through December 31 regardless of issue date.
- Stay **25 feet** from any building or structure; pile no larger than 3 ft × 3 ft.
- **Fires must be out by 1:00 a.m.**

Violating these rules may result in a revoked permit, suspended burning privileges, and
being charged for the cost of the fire run. Any property loss from your burn is your
responsibility.
