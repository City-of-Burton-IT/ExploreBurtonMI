## Boards, commissions & committees

The City of Burton regularly needs residents to join our **Boards, Commissions, and Committees** &mdash;
there may be an open position that's right for you. Call **[(810) 743-1500](tel:+18107431500)** for more
information.

## Volunteer & community service

![Welcome volunteers](/welcomevolunteer.png)

Looking for ways to give back to your community? These local organizations welcome volunteers:

- **Beautify Burton** &mdash; local cleanup efforts (highway overpasses) and community gardening. Contact City Councilman Kris Johns at [(810) 348-4437](tel:+18103484437) or [flowers@beautifyburton.com](mailto:flowers@beautifyburton.com).
- **Food Bank of Eastern Michigan** &mdash; a major hub for food-related volunteering. [(810) 239-4441](tel:+18102394441)
- **Volunteers of America Michigan (Burton Thrift Store)** &mdash; support homeless veterans and families. Volunteer coordinator: [(248) 204-6042](tel:+12482046042) or [volunteers@voami.org](mailto:volunteers@voami.org).
- **Habitat for Humanity** &mdash; housing projects and ReStore operations. 101 Burton St, Flint, MI 48503 &mdash; [(810) 766-9089](tel:+18107669089).
- **Seeds of Love** &mdash; a free clothing closet at Open Door United Methodist Church, 4010 Lippincott Blvd. Tuesdays 12:00&ndash;2:00 p.m. (appointments also available).
- **For-Mar Nature Preserve & Arboretum** &mdash; nature-focused educational outreach. 2142 N. Genesee Road &mdash; [(810) 789-8567](tel:+18107898567).
- **Burton Senior Center** &mdash; social activities and volunteering for older residents. 3410 S. Grand Traverse &mdash; [(810) 744-0960](tel:+18107440960).
- **United Way of Genesee County** &mdash; coordinates fundraising for children's basic needs. 111 E Court St, Suite 3A, Flint &mdash; [(810) 232-8121](tel:+18102328121).
- **Genesee County Humane Society** &mdash; animal welfare and community education. G-3325 S. Dort Hwy &mdash; [(810) 744-0511](tel:+18107440511) &mdash; [geneseehumane.org](https://www.geneseehumane.org/).
- **Big Brothers Big Sisters of Greater Flint** &mdash; youth mentorship. 1176 Robert T. Longway Blvd, Flint &mdash; [(810) 235-0617](tel:+18102350617) or [info@bbbsflint.org](mailto:info@bbbsflint.org).
- **Burton Race Series** &mdash; a volunteer-driven lineup of three runs/walks held throughout the year. Contact Tony Newton at [(810) 869-1284](tel:+18108691284).

You can also engage with local community groups (such as the "Burton City" group on Facebook) to find
immediate needs, and watch the city's **Events** for ways to sponsor or take part.

## Clubs & organizations

Burton has a strong network of civic and service organizations. Some smaller clubs operate under larger
regional umbrellas (like the Flint & Genesee Chamber) but remain deeply active in the community.

### Business & civic leadership

- **Burton Chamber of Commerce** &mdash; business networking, community events (Memorial Day Parade), and local economic growth. 4303 S Center Rd (in City Hall) &mdash; [(810) 743-1500](tel:+18107431500) &mdash; [burtonchamber.org](https://burtonchamber.org).
- **Flint & Genesee Chamber** &mdash; regional business advocacy that supports Burton businesses. 519 S. Saginaw St, Flint &mdash; [(810) 600-1404](tel:+18106001404).

### Service clubs

- **Rotary Club of Burton** &mdash; community service, youth scholarships, and fundraisers. Meets Thursdays 12:15 p.m. at Sharky's Sports Bar (4050 S. Center Rd) &mdash; [burtonrotary.org](https://burtonrotary.org).
- **Kiwanis Club of Burton** &mdash; helping local children and families; known for the annual "Hot Fudge 5K." [(810) 210-4007](tel:+18102104007).
- **Greater Flint Area Arbor #766 (Gleaner Life)** &mdash; a fraternal service club supporting local youth and elderly programs (gleanerlife.org).

### Fraternal & veteran organizations

- **Fraternal Order of Eagles (Aerie #3814)** &mdash; 3317 E Bristol Rd &mdash; [(810) 742-1020](tel:+18107421020).
- **VFW Post 2777 (Veterans of Foreign Wars)** &mdash; 2260 S Belsay Rd &mdash; [(810) 743-4138](tel:+18107434138).
- **American Legion** &mdash; there is no Burton post building currently; many residents are active in Post 413 (Grand Blanc) or Post 267 (Davison).

### Community support

- **Old Newsboys of Flint** &mdash; clothing and support for children in need (Burton is a primary service area). 6255 Taylor Dr, Flint &mdash; [(810) 744-1840](tel:+18107441840).
