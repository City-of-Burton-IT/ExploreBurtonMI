## Getting around by bus

Public transit in Burton is provided by the **Mass Transportation Authority (MTA)** of Genesee
County, based in Flint. MTA's fixed bus routes connect Burton with Flint and neighboring
communities, and connect to the Flint transit center for transfers across the county.

### Routes serving Burton

- **[Route 15 — Burton–Davison](https://www.mtaflint.org/primary-routes/primary-route-15-burton-davison/)**
- **[Route 9 — Lapeer Road](https://www.mtaflint.org/primary-routes/primary-route-9-lapeer-road/)**
- **[Route 11 — Fenton Road](https://www.mtaflint.org/primary-routes/primary-route-11-fenton-road/)**

Each route page has the current schedule and stops. Routes and times can change, so check the
official MTA page before you travel. You can also see every MTA route that runs through Burton on
the **Map** — open the layer control (top-right) and turn on **Bus routes**.
