Curbside service is provided by **EMTERRA** — 1606 E. Webster Rd., Flint, MI 48505 ·
(810) 667-4885. Garbage, recycling, and yard waste are all collected on the **same day**,
and everything must be at the edge of the road (outside the ditch line) by **6:00 a.m.**

## Garbage

- Unlimited bags within reason, **35 gallons or less**, properly contained and tied.
  No small grocery bags (they're treated as loose trash).
- Trash must be **bagged**, not loose in containers.
- In winter, don't let cans fill with water — frozen bags can't be collected.
- Each household gets **one free dump permit per month** (see Permits).
- You may set out **one large item per week** on your normal trash day if it's **under
  50 lbs and two people can lift it**. A whole-house clean-out requires a dump permit
  from City Hall.

**Accepted large items:** couches, chairs, tables, TVs, washers, dryers, stoves, box
springs (fabric items must be dry — cover with clear plastic if raining); dried-out
**latex** paint cans (lid off); carpet (rolled/tied, under 4 ft, 45 lbs or less, max 8
rolls/week); clean, bug-free mattresses (bagged if dirty or wet). Refrigerators, freezers,
and A/C units are collected once **Freon is removed** by a licensed company and tagged
with the removal date.

**Not accepted:** oil paint (hazardous), chest freezers, cast iron tubs, cement/concrete/
rocks, tires, nails or screws, and demolition lumber. Bundle material no larger than
4 ft × 1 ft; larger amounts are industrial refuse and must be removed by the resident or
their contractor.

## Recycling

Picked up the **same day** as trash, out by **6:00 a.m.** The City sells recycling
containers for **$7.00** each, and offers **free stickers** to use your own lidded/handled
container (35 gallons max, under 50 lbs). Place recycling on the opposite side of the
driveway from the trash, label facing the road. **No plastic bags.**

## Yard Waste

Collected the **same day** as trash, **first full week of April through the last full week
of November** (weather permitting). Use **two-ply paper yard-waste bags** (from grocery or
hardware stores) or your own 35-gallon (under 50 lb) container with **free yard-waste
stickers** from City Hall (limit four per household). **No plastic bags.**

**Includes:** lawn clippings, brush, thatch, leaves, border edgings, tree & plant pruning,
vines & garden clearings. Brush/branches: under 2 inches in diameter, bundled in 4 ft
lengths. **Christmas trees** are collected through the end of January (bare — no ornaments
or hooks). **Not yard waste:** gravel, dirt, sod, rock, stones, stumps, cement.

## Bulk Items

**One bulk item per week**, collected the same day as trash. Examples: stoves, A/C units,
refrigerators, bed frames, hot water heaters, water softeners, garbage disposals, freezers,
dishwashers, dryers, humidifiers, treadmills, washers. **Freon** appliances need the Freon
removed by a licensed company and tagged with the date.

- **Cardboard:** flattened, bundled, tied with twine; no longer/wider than 3 ft.
- **Computers:** one complete unit (monitor, desktop, printer) free; extra or larger
  office equipment over 50 lbs is handled as a bulk item.
- **Tires & auto parts:** **not accepted.** For tires, contact Environmental Rubber
  Recycling Inc. at (810) 789-1222.
- **Non-collectible:** animal waste, dead animals, lumber, demolition material,
  insecticides, oil/gas/propane tanks, antifreeze, concrete, gravel, dirt, sod, rock,
  stumps, car parts, batteries, riding mowers, bricks, roofing shingles, and paint cans
  with paint still in them.

## Hazardous & Electronic Waste

Handled by the **Genesee County Metropolitan Planning Commission**. Check dates and
locations at (810) 762-7744 or [gcmpc.org](http://www.gcmpc.org). Anything labeled toxic,
corrosive, flammable, or reactive is hazardous and needs special disposal.

## 2026 Holiday Collection Schedule

When a holiday falls on or before your collection day, service is **delayed by one day**
that week:

- **New Year's Day** — Thursday, January 1, 2026
- **Memorial Day** — Monday, May 25, 2026
- **Independence Day** — Saturday, July 4, 2026
- **Labor Day** — Monday, September 7, 2026
- **Thanksgiving Day** — Thursday, November 26, 2026
- **Christmas Day** — Friday, December 25, 2026
