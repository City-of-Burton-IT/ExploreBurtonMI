Explore Burton — Privacy Policy

City of Burton, Michigan

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Explore Burton is a free, public information service from the City of Burton, Michigan — an interactive map of city businesses, government facilities, and services, plus plain-language data dashboards and a Resident Guide. It is available as a website (explore.burtonmi.gov) and as a mobile app. This policy explains what information the website and app do and do not handle.

The short version: We don't ask you to sign in, and we don't collect personal information about you. There are no advertisements, no tracking cookies, and we never sell or share data about visitors. The app's “Near me” feature uses your location only on your device, and only when you tap it.

Information we do not collect

  • No accounts or passwords — there is nothing to sign up for. (If you choose to submit a listing change request, you provide contact details for that request only — see “Listing change requests” below.)
  • No advertising, ad networks, or advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking cookies and no cross-site or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share information about visitors with anyone.

Location (“Near me”)

The map has an optional “Near me” button that centers the map on you and sorts nearby places by distance. When you tap it, your device asks your permission and provides a single location reading that is used only on your device to position the map. Your location is not collected, stored, transmitted, or shared, and the app does not track your location in the background. If you never tap “Near me,” the app never requests your location. The Android app declares the standard coarse and fine location permissions solely for this on-device feature.

Listing change requests

The app has an optional “Suggest an edit” / “Add a business” form for correcting or adding map listings. If you use it, we ask for your name, a phone number or email, and your relationship to the business. This contact information is used only by City of Burton staff to verify the request. It is stored in the City’s internal systems, is never published, and is not used for marketing or shared outside the City. The business details you submit appear on the public map only after a City employee reviews and approves them.

Issue reports (“Report an issue”)

The map has an optional “Report an issue” form for telling the City about potholes, damaged signs, drainage problems, and streetlights. Your report — the location you mark, your description, and any photo you attach — goes to the City’s internal public-works queue for triage. Reports are not published. Providing your name and contact information is optional; if given, it is used only if staff need to follow up and is never published or used for marketing. Photos are resized on your device before upload. This form is not an emergency line — call 911 for emergencies.

When you drop a pin, the form may suggest the nearest street address to help you describe the location. This lookup happens entirely on your device against a list of City addresses bundled with the app. The spot you mark is not sent to Google, OpenStreetMap, or any other outside service. The address is only a suggestion; you can edit it, and nothing is sent to the City until you submit the report.

Push notifications (Android app)

The Android app lets you opt in to push notifications for City alerts, service disruptions, and meeting reminders (Settings → Notifications). This is off until you turn it on and grant Android’s notification permission. Notifications are delivered by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging using topic subscriptions: your device joins a topic (for example, “alerts”) anonymously. The City does not receive or store a device token, and there is no per-resident record of who is subscribed — the City only sends a message to a topic, and Google delivers it to whichever devices are subscribed. You can turn any category off in the app or in your device settings at any time. Push is not used for advertising.

Aggregate website analytics

To understand overall usage (for example, which pages are popular), the website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first measurement tool that is cookieless and does not use fingerprinting or any client-side identifier to follow individuals. It reports only aggregate, non-identifying metrics such as page views, referrers, general device type, and country-level region. It does not build a profile of you. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for how Cloudflare handles aggregate data.

On-device storage

The website may store a single setting in your browser to remember that you dismissed the “Add to home screen” prompt. It is a simple yes/no flag, stays on your device, contains no personal information, and is not used for tracking.

Information shown in the app

The places, boundaries, and dashboard figures shown in Explore Burton come from public and City-owned sources (for example U.S. Census data, state and county open-data portals, and the City's own records). These describe the community, not its visitors. The app loads this content from explore.burtonmi.gov and keeps a bundled copy for offline use.

Hosting and server logs

The site is delivered through GitHub Pages and the Cloudflare content network. As with any website, their servers process standard technical request information (such as your IP address and the page requested) to deliver pages and protect the service. That handling is governed by those providers' policies; the City does not operate its own visitor-tracking on top of it.

Links to other sites

Explore Burton links to outside resources (for example transit schedules, county services, and state tools). Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover them.

Children's privacy

Explore Burton is a general-audience public information service and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Explore Burton can be directed to the City of Burton, Michigan at privacy@burtonmi.gov, or via burtonmi.gov.
