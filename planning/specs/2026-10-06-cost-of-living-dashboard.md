# What It Costs to Live Here -- Design Spec

Issue #23. Decision 2026-10-06: build it, framed as an honest "what it costs to
live here" view that shows income and rent burden beside housing costs. It is
not an "affordable" pitch; the data check on the issue showed Burton's housing
costs far below national medians but household income 26% below too, with 44%
of renters still cost-burdened.

## Purpose

Give a resident or a prospective resident one page that answers "what does it
cost to live in Burton, and how does that compare," without hiding the income
side. Every cost figure sits next to the Flint metro, Michigan, and the nation,
and every Burton figure carries its margin of error.

## Scope (v1)

- Dashboard id `costofliving`, label "What It Costs to Live Here", in the
  Money & Taxes group ahead of City Finances.
- Six stats with benchmarks: median home value, median gross rent, owner costs
  with a mortgage, median household income, renters spending 30% or more on
  rent, Flint metro price level (BEA RPP, US = 100).
- Three charts: compare chart (Burton, Flint metro, Michigan, US) for the five
  ACS measures; bars for the metro price level by category; trend lines for
  the metro and state price level over the last five data years.
- An explainer: what RPP is, what cost-burdened means, why income is on a
  cost dashboard, how to read margins of error.

## Out of scope (v1)

- Any place-level price index (none exists; RPP stops at the metro).
- Utility, transportation, food or childcare costs: no clean public-domain
  place-level source. Transportation burden already lives in the Affordability
  & Access dashboard (H+T, modeled).
- City-sourced figures from BS&A (taxable values, sales, utility bills).
  Candidate for v2; see "Dependencies / open items".

## Data model

`public/info-costofliving.json`, generated by `tools/fetch_costofliving.py`:

- ACS 5-year (2024 vintage, 2020-2024): B19013 income, B25064 gross rent,
  B25077 home value, B25088_002 owner costs with a mortgage, B25070 rent burden
  (rows 007-010 over 001 minus 011). Geographies: place 26-12060, MSA 22420,
  state 26, us.
- BEA Regional Price Parities 2024 for the Flint MSA and Michigan: all items,
  goods, housing (rents), other services. Read from the FRED text mirrors, or
  from `tools/data/bea-rpp.json` when FRED is unreachable from the build host
  (it was on 2026-10-06; the file records the source URLs and copy date).

Resident-facing copy (headline, summary, responsibility, action, takeaways,
sections, priorities) lives in `public/dashboard-clarity.json` under
`costofliving` and is hand-written.

## Honesty (ADR-0001)

- Headline leads with the trade-off, not the discount.
- RPP is labelled metro-wide on the stat, the chart, and the explainer.
- Margins of error are shown on every Burton stat hint.
- Rent-burden shares exclude households whose burden the Census could not
  compute, and the note says so.
- Nominal dollars; the note says so.

## City records (v2, shipped 2026-10-06)

`tools/Export-BsaCostOfLiving.ps1` runs on the IT workstation that holds the
read-only BS&A credential (`C:\utils\BsaSql`, db_datareader on the Assessing
database) and writes `tools/data/bsa-residential.json`: residential parcel
count, median SEV and taxable value, principal-residence-exemption count, and
the median arm's-length sale price per calendar year (assessor terms code 03,
price over $10,000). The fetch tool reads it with `--city-file` (default when
the file exists), validates the shape at the boundary, and adds two stats
("Typical home sale price" benchmarked against the Census estimate, "Homes
with a principal residence exemption"), a sales trend chart, two explainer
items, and a note. Aggregates only, never parcel-level.

## Dependencies / open items

- Typical water and sewer bill needs a read-only grant on the Utility Billing
  database before it can join the City-records section.
- Housing and Demographics were refreshed to the 2024 ACS vintage alongside
  this work so the three panels agree on home value and rent.

## Refresh recipe

```text
CENSUS_API_KEY=... python tools/fetch_costofliving.py --year 2025 --rpp-year 2025
```

Update `tools/data/bea-rpp.json` first if FRED is still unreachable, re-run
`tools/Export-BsaCostOfLiving.ps1 -Database 'D001City Of Burton <year>'` on the
workstation with the BS&A credential, then bump `public/freshness.json` and
re-read the clarity copy for stale numbers.
